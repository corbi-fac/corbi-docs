Saves and Forks

Every answered prompt already leaves a saved .blend and a timestamped chat history, so we don't need a bookmarks table. A save is the checkpoint, a timestamp ties it to its conversation, and carrying on from an older save starts a new session.

Status, 24 Nov 2026. The session half is adopted: ADR-010 lists every session, makes name its title and removes the bookmark calls, and ADR-009 is superseded (#167). Save markers, the list-saves endpoint and forking from an older save are not built. The graph diagrams below show the harness as it was on 17 November; the feedback node has since been removed and the run streams its events directly. A fifth table, attachments, now also hangs off sessions. See the architecture page for the current graph.
